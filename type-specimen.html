On Ballooning . . .

In the dawn's gentle embrace, we rise,
A hot-air balloon in the painted skies.
Colors dance in the morning light,
Floating softly, a graceful flight.
Whispers of wind guide our way,
Over fields where shadows play.
Above the world, in silence we roam,
In the sky's vast expanse, we find our home.

This simple type specimen was made by placing the balloon image as the background of a <div>. Type was placed inside the div. The complementary fonts were chosen by ChatGPT, which suggested “Dancing Script” for <h1> and “Merriweather” for <p>. Alternate lines were indented using the case-sensitive &Tab; character, which requires the styles “white-space: pre-wrap;” and “ and “tab-size: 6;” (where 6 is the number of characters to indent).
