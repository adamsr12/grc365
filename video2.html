Balloon Federation of America

This tutorial showed how to place a video in a web page and how to create four types of links (relative/local, absolute/global, anchor, and email).

The Balloon Federation of America [absolute or global link to another site] is a 501(c)3 non-profit association dedicated to the advancement of the sport and science of lighter-than-air aviation, both hot air and gas balloons. Founded in 1961, membership in the BFA is open to anyone who shares a fascination with Lighter Than Air flight.
